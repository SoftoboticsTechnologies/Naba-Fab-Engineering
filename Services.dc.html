What we supply

Labour services

Skilled and unskilled manpower for erection, maintenance, shutdown and general plant work, deployed shift-wise or contract-wise.

Deployments range from small single-shift gangs for routine maintenance to larger crews mobilised together for planned shutdowns. We work both on a per-shift basis and on multi-month shutdown contracts, covering general labour, riggers, helpers and shutdown gangs.

Skilled Unskilled Shutdown crews

Machine operators

Trained operators for pouch machines, presses and packaging lines, along with automation-supported process equipment.

Assignments run from a single operator on shift-basis relief cover to multi-operator teams supporting continuous production runs. We take on both short-term postings and longer contract engagements on pouch machines, wax presses, filter presses and packaging lines.

Technical services

Mechanical tradesmen for fabrication, erection and maintenance jobs, including insulation and coating work.

Crews are supplied as individual tradesmen for routine maintenance or as full fabrication gangs for shutdown and erection contracts. Trades covered include welders, fitters, mechanical hands, insulation applicators and fibre glass coating technicians.

FAQ

Common questions

How many workers can you mobilise?

Crew size is scaled to the job — from a handful of skilled hands for routine maintenance to larger gangs for planned shutdowns.

Can you take on single-day jobs?

Yes. We supply crews for single-shift and single-day requirements as well as longer, multi-month shutdown contracts.

What is your service area?

We primarily serve refineries, oil mills, solvent extraction and chemical plants across West Bengal, based out of Kapasdanga, Hooghly.

Do your crews carry safety gear/PPE?

Yes. All crews are supplied with standard PPE and work under supervision following a safety-first approach on every site.
