01 — ABOUT US

A contractor the plants in Hooghly call back.

Naba Fab Engineering was established in 2002 at Kapasdanga, Hooghly, and has grown into a trusted industrial services provider across West Bengal. We started with fabrication and maintenance crews for local oil mills and refineries, and today deploy skilled and unskilled labour, machine operators and technical manpower across solvent extraction, hydrogenation, refinery and packaging operations.

Work is carried out under direct supervision with a safety-first discipline, whether it is a shutdown job on a tank vessel, chimney and structure work at height, or a running-shift operator requirement. We are a registered business with GST and PAN on record, and we invoice and document accordingly.
